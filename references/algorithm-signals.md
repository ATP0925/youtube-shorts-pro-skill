# Platform Algorithm Signals

> Understanding what the algorithm measures is the difference between 100 views and 10M views.

---

## YouTube Shorts Algorithm

YouTube Shorts uses a **tiered distribution model** independent from long-form content. Performance in Shorts does NOT affect long-form recommendations.

### Primary Signals (Ranked by Weight)

#### 1. Swipe-Away Rate (The Primary Gatekeeper)
- **What:** Percentage of users who immediately scroll past your Short
- **Studio Metric:** "How many chose to view" (Viewed vs. Swiped Away)
- **Target:** > 70% viewed / < 30% swiped away
- **Impact:** HIGH — This is the ultimate first filter. High swipe-away = immediate throttle.
- **Fix:** Unmistakable visual + caption hook in < 2 seconds

#### 2. Completion Rate / Average Percentage Viewed
- **What:** Share of the Short watched before exiting
- **Studio Metric:** Average percentage viewed
- **Target:** > 90% on 20-35s clips
- **Impact:** HIGH — Unlike long-form (where total watch minutes matter), Shorts favor completion rate
- **Fix:** Eliminate dead air, tight pacing, loop ending into opening frame

#### 3. Loop Rate / Replay Rate
- **What:** Whether a viewer watches the Short a second time
- **Target:** Any loop > 100% average view duration
- **Impact:** VERY HIGH — One of the strongest positive indicators
- **Fix:** Structure ending to seamlessly loop back to beginning

#### 4. Shares (High-Intent Engagement)
- **What:** Viewer shares the Short externally or via DM
- **Impact:** VERY HIGH — Shares carry significantly more weight than likes/comments. A share = viewer attaches their personal reputation to your content
- **Fix:** Create content that triggers "you have to see this" reactions

#### 5. Saves
- **What:** Viewer saves the Short to a playlist or Saved Shorts
- **Impact:** HIGH — Signals utility, re-watchability, or high perceived value
- **Fix:** Educational content, rare discoveries, beautiful edits worth revisiting

#### 6. Likes & Comments
- **What:** Standard engagement metrics
- **Impact:** MODERATE — Likes are the easiest signal; comments show deeper engagement
- **Fix:** End with a question CTA. Use pinned owner comment for discussion prompt.

#### 7. Subscribe Actions
- **What:** Viewer subscribes from the Shorts player
- **Impact:** MODERATE — Signals channel-level interest beyond one video
- **Fix:** Consistent niche quality; don't beg for subs

### View Count Definition
YouTube counts a Shorts view instantly when the video starts to play or replay (no minimum watch-time threshold for raw views). "Engaged views" have different thresholds for monetization.

### Distribution Tiers
```
Post → Small Test Batch (200-500 users)
  → If retention/engagement passes threshold:
    → Medium Batch (5K-50K users)
      → If still strong:
        → Large Batch (500K-5M users)
          → Viral tier (10M+ users)
```

### Format Rules
- Shorts can be up to **3 minutes** long
- Vertical (9:16) is mandatory for Shorts feed placement
- Shorts-specific hashtag `#Shorts` helps discovery but is not required

## Instagram Reels Algorithm

Instagram Reels bridges social graph (followers/sharing) with algorithmic recommendations.

### Primary Signals (Ranked by Weight)

#### 1. Watch Time & Completion Rate
- Identical importance as YouTube Shorts
- Full watch-throughs and replays are the top signal
- Short, tight content (15-30s) often outperforms longer Reels for completion

#### 2. Sends / Shares / DMs
- **THE #1 UNIQUE IG SIGNAL** — Instagram heavily weights private shares via DMs
- When a Reel gets shared between friends, it receives an enormous algorithmic boost
- This is the key differentiator from YouTube Shorts

#### 3. Saves
- Strong signal for utility and educational content
- Saves > Likes in algorithmic weight

#### 4. Re-watches
- Signals high content quality and emotional impact
- Loops and satisfying edits drive re-watches

#### 5. Comments
- Meaningful comment threads (not just emojis) drive testing
- Creator-to-audience reply interaction = community signal

#### 6. Audio Trend Alignment
- Using trending audio tracks boosts Reels into audio-based discovery
- Even at low volume (1-5%), trending audio registers in the recommendation engine

### Negative Signals (Content Suppressors)
| Signal | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| TikTok watermark | Algorithmically suppressed |
| Recycled/reposted content | Distribution throttled |
| Low-resolution video | Deprioritized vs. HD |
| Text-heavy thumbnails (spam look) | Reduced reach |
| Inconsistent niche switching | Confuses audience modeling |

## TikTok Algorithm

TikTok uses a pure interest-graph model (not social graph) for the For You Page.

### Primary Signals

#### 1. Watch Time & Completion Rate
- Same as other platforms — the universal king signal
- TikTok tests with very small batches first (50-200 users)

#### 2. Re-watches / Loops
- Strongest positive signal on TikTok specifically
- 15s videos that loop 3-4 times generate massive retention metrics

#### 3. Shares
- External shares (text, other apps) weighted higher than in-app shares

#### 4. Comments & Comment Quality
- Long, discussion-driving comments signal content depth
- Controversial/debate-sparking content often gets pushed harder

#### 5. Follows from Video
- A follow directly from a video signals very high content-channel fit

#### 6. Content Signals (Semantic)
- TikTok parses **spoken words** (via auto-captions), **on-screen text**, and **audio profiles** for keyword matching
- This makes TikTok function as a search engine for Gen Z
- Optimize text/captions for searchable keywords

### TikTok-Specific Rules
- Reposted/unoriginal content is systematically suppressed
- Cross-posted content with other platform watermarks is throttled
- Original content with original audio gets distribution priority

## Optimal Video Duration by Platform

| Platform | Sweet Spot | Max Shorts/Reels |
|----------|------------|-----------------|
| YouTube Shorts | 20-35 seconds | 180 seconds |
| Instagram Reels | 15-30 seconds | 90 seconds |
| TikTok | 15-30 seconds | 180 seconds |

> **Rule of Thumb:** Make every second count. A tight 25s video with 95% completion crushes a padded 55s video with 40% completion.

## The Anti-Pattern List (Things That Kill Distribution)

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Kills | All Platforms? |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|
| Watermark from other platform | Explicit algorithmic penalty | ✓ |
| Black/blank opening frames | Instant swipe-away | ✓ |
| Inconsistent niche switching | Breaks audience model | ✓ |
| Begging for engagement | Feels spammy, reduces organic action | ✓ |
| Recycled/reposted content | Originality penalty | ✓ |
| Hashtag spam (#fyp #viral) | No benefit, looks desperate | ✓ |
| Low-resolution uploads | Quality preference in ranking | ✓ |
| Silent or near-silent audio | Broken content signal | ✓ |
| Landscape in portrait frame | Low engagement, poor mobile experience | ✓ |
