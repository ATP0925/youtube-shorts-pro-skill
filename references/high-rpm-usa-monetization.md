# High-RPM USA / Tier-1 Monetization & Legal AdSense Strategy

## Viewer Geography Controls RPM (Not Passport Spoofing)

| Metric / Setting | What Determines It | Best Practice |
|---|---|---|
| **RPM & CPM (`$` Rate)** | **Where your viewers watch from** (USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany earn highest RPM) | Target US English Shorts, `English (United States)` metadata, and US EST/PST upload hours |
| **AdSense Country / Territory** | **Your legal banking & tax residence** (needed for PIN verification, PAN/govt ID, SWIFT payouts) | **Always set to your true home country** (e.g., `India`). Never fake `United States` in AdSense! |
| **US Withholding Tax** | IRS Form **`W-8BEN`** submitted inside AdSense Tax Info | Reduces US viewer ad withholding from `30%` down to `15%` under India–US DTAA treaty |

## YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Official Milestones

### Tier 1 — Fan Funding (Memberships, Super Chat, Shopping)
- **500 subscribers**
- **3 valid public uploads** in the last 90 days
- Plus **3,000 public watch hours** (last 365 days) **OR 3 million valid Shorts views** (last 90 days)

### Tier 2 — Full Ad Revenue Sharing (Watch Page + Shorts Feed Ads)
- **1,000 subscribers**
- Plus **4,000 public watch hours** (last 365 days) **OR 10 million valid Shorts views** (last 90 days)
- Plus 2-Step Verification + active AdSense account + passing Reused/Inauthentic Content human review.

## Signals That Lock Your Channel Into the US Gaming Feed
1. **`Settings -> Upload defaults -> Advanced settings`:**
   - `Category`: `Gaming`
   - `Video language`: `English (United States)`
   - `Title and description language`: `English (United States)`
2. **Official Game Title Tagging:** Set `Game title (optional)` -> `Grand Theft Auto VI` (or your game) per upload so Shorts join US game shelves.
3. **Clean Language Feed:** Zero mixed-language uploads (e.g., keep Hindi/local clips on a separate IG/regional channel).
4. **EST/PST Prime-Time Uploads:** Schedule publishing for `9:00 AM EST` & `12:15 PM EST` / `5:00 PM EST` US feed waves.
