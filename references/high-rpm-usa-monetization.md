## YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Milestones & Typical Timelines

### Tier 1 — Fan Funding (Memberships, Super Chat, Shopping)
- **Requirements:** `500` subscribers + `3` valid public uploads (last 90 days) + (`3,000` watch hours in 365 days **OR** `3M` valid Shorts views in 90 days)
- **Typical Pace (2 daily pro Shorts):** Often reachable in **30–90 days** if at least 2–3 clips break out into `500K+` Shorts shelf waves.

### Tier 2 — Full Ad Revenue Sharing (Shorts Feed + Watch Ads)
- **Requirements:** `1,000` subscribers + (`4,000` watch hours in 365 days **OR** `10M` valid Shorts views in 90 days)
- **Prerequisites:** 2-Step Verification active, approved AdSense account matching legal country + tax `W-8BEN`, and passing human **Reused / Inauthentic Content** review.
- **Typical Pace:** Often **60–180 days** depending on breakout viral frequency.

## Signals That Lock Your Channel Into the US Gaming Feed
1. **`Settings -> Upload defaults -> Advanced settings`:**
   - `Category`: `Gaming`
   - `Video language`: `English (United States)`
   - `Title and description language`: `English (United States)`
2. **Official Game Title Tagging:** Set `Game title (optional)` -> `Grand Theft Auto VI` (or your target game) per upload so Shorts join official game shelves.
3. **Strict Language Isolation:** Zero mixed-language uploads (route Hinglish/regional clips to Instagram Reels or a separate channel).
4. **EST/PST Prime-Time Publishing:** Target `9:00 AM EST` & `12:15 PM EST` feed waves consistently.
